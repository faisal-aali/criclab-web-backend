# What CricLab is

CricLab is a cricket performance lab you use from your phone. You film a
delivery, upload the clip, and get back measured numbers about that delivery
along with a coaching read of what they mean.

It is built for bowlers, coaches, academies and clubs who want the kind of
feedback that used to need a lab booking and a specialist.

## Two kinds of clip

CricLab has two analysis modes. They measure different things and must not be
mixed:

- **Action** (Video Analysis) is filmed **side-on**. It reads the body: arm
  speed, release, front-foot contact, stride and the bowling action. Use this
  when you want mechanics.
- **Ball flight** is filmed from behind the bowler, with both sets of stumps
  in frame. It reads the ball in the air: speed, line and length, bounce. Use
  this when you want where the ball went.

A clip filmed for one mode will not produce the other mode's numbers. If you
want both reads, film two clips.

## What you get from one clip

- Ball speed at release, and how it changed through the flight (when the ball
  is visible in the air)
- Where the ball pitched — line and length, plotted on the pitch (Ball flight)
- Run-up and delivery-stride timing
- Front foot contact and the exact moment of release
- Arm speed and rotation through the delivery swing
- Body angles at the key moments: back foot contact, front foot contact, release
- A written read of what stood out, and what to work on next
- Suggested drills from the library, matched to what the report found

Finished reports live in History so you can compare this month against last.
The Leaderboard ranks the twenty fastest Action throws on the platform by
measured ball speed. Other players' full reports stay private — you only see
name, speed, and whether a throw is yours.

## How to start

1. Confirm your email if you have not already.
2. Read the filming guide so the camera angle matches the mode you want.
3. Open Video Analysis for an Action clip, or Ball Flight for a line-and-length
   clip, and upload.
4. Wait while the clip is processed — you will see progress and a time estimate.
5. Open the report. Come back to History any time.

You can also book a coach from Coaching and attach a delivery so the session is
about a real clip, not a description.

## What CricLab is not

CricLab is a coaching tool. It is not officiating equipment and it does not
replace an accredited biomechanics assessment. Nothing it reports should be
used to rule on the legality of an action in a match — a single camera cannot
carry that weight, and CricLab will tell you when a question is beyond what
your footage can answer.

## Honest numbers

If a measurement cannot be made reliably from the clip you uploaded, CricLab
says so and explains why. It never fills the gap with a guess. A missing number
with a reason is more useful than a confident wrong one. Film side-on for
Action, or use Ball flight (behind the bowler, both wickets) when you need
broadcast-style speed, line and length.
