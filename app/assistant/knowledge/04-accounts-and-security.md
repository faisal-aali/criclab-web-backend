# Your account

## Creating an account

Sign up with your name, email and a password of at least eight characters
including a letter and a number. CricLab emails you a six-digit code to confirm
the address. The code is valid for ten minutes and you can ask for a new one.

You need a confirmed email address before you can upload footage or book a
coaching session.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email and password. Your session stays active across visits.
If you have not used CricLab for a long time you will be asked to sign in again.

## Forgotten password

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. If the address has an account, a
reset code arrives by email. The code expires after ten minutes, can be used
once, and stops working as soon as a new one is requested.

For your protection, the page gives the same reply whether or not the address
has an account.

## Changing your password

Change it from Account settings. You will need your current password. Changing
it signs you out on every other device — the one you are using stays signed in.

## Where you are signed in

Account settings lists your active sessions with the device and when it was last
used. You can end any of them, or sign out everywhere at once. Do that if you
have used a shared computer or think someone else has your password.

## Email notifications

CricLab emails you about things that happen on your account: confirming your
address, password changes, new sign-ins, support ticket replies and coaching
session confirmations, changes and reminders.

## Your footage

Your clips and reports are yours. They are visible only to you unless you share
them. You can delete a session from your history at any time, and deleting it
removes the footage as well.
