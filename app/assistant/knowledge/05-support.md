# Getting help

## Opening a ticket

Support lives in the app under Support. Open a ticket with a subject, a
description and, if it helps, up to four attachments — screenshots, a PDF or a
short clip, up to 12MB each.

Pick the category that fits: analysis or metrics, something broken, account and
sign-in, billing, coaching sessions, feedback, or something else. Set the
priority honestly — "blocking my session" is for when you cannot get on with
what you were doing.

## What happens next

You get an email confirming the ticket with its reference, which looks like
`CL-7KQ2MN`. When support replies you get an email and a notification in the
app, and the ticket appears with an unread marker.

A ticket sits in one of a few states. **Open** means it is logged and waiting to
be picked up. **Awaiting support** means you replied last. **Awaiting you**
means support replied and it is with you. **Resolved** means it is done.

## Reopening

Replying to a resolved ticket reopens it, for up to three weeks after it was
resolved. After that, open a new one and mention the old reference.

## Things worth including

- The reference of the delivery or session you are asking about
- What you expected and what you saw instead
- A screenshot, if it is a display problem
- The clip, if it is an analysis problem — though CricLab usually already has it
