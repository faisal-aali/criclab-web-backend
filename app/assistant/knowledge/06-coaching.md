# Coaching sessions

## Booking

Coaching lives in the app under Coaching. Pick a coach, pick the kind of session
you want, and pick a time from their calendar. Times are shown in the coach's
timezone, labelled clearly.

Every coach offers a short clip review and a longer full session. If you have
not worked with a coach before, the clip review is the sensible first booking.

You need a confirmed email address to book. You can hold up to six upcoming
sessions at once.

## Before the session

Upload a clip beforehand if you can. A session with footage to look at is worth
several without. You can attach a delivery reference to the booking so the coach
knows which one you want to talk about.

## Changing or cancelling

You can cancel or move a session up to twelve hours before it starts, from your
sessions list. Inside twelve hours, open a support ticket and it will be sorted
out.

Moving a session books the new time first and releases the old one afterwards,
so you never lose your slot to a failed change.

## Reminders

You get an email and a notification the day before a session.

## What the coaches cover

Coaches on CricLab specialise: fast bowling mechanics and run-up work, spin and
the biomechanics behind it, and workload and season planning. Each profile lists
what they focus on and which languages they work in.
