# Plans and billing

CricLab has a free tier for people trying it out, and paid plans for players and
coaches who use it regularly. Pricing, what is included at each level and any
current offers are on the Pricing page of the website — that page is always the
authority, and prices there may have moved since anything else was written.

## Common questions

**Can I try it before paying?** Yes. The free tier lets you analyse deliveries
and see the full report.

**Do coaching sessions come out of my plan?** Coaching sessions are booked
separately from your plan.

**Team and academy access.** Academies and clubs are catered for. Get in touch
through the contact page or open a support ticket under Billing, and it will be
set up for you.

**Refunds and cancellations.** Open a support ticket under Billing. Include what
you were charged and when.

For anything about an actual charge on your account, open a support ticket
rather than asking the assistant — the assistant cannot see your billing.
