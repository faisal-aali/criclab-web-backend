# When something does not work

## "The ball was not tracked"

Almost always the camera angle or the light.

- Filming from behind the bowler is the main cause. The ball has to travel
  *across* the frame, not away from it. Move square to the pitch.
- Poor light smears a fast ball into a streak. Film in daylight or under proper
  floodlights.
- A busy background — netting, spectators, a bright sky right behind the ball —
  makes the ball hard to separate. Shift your angle a few metres.
- A low frame rate on a fast bowler leaves too few frames of the ball in flight.
  Use 60fps or higher.

## "The speed looks too low"

Check the clip is filmed side-on. A ball travelling towards or away from the
camera reads slower than it is. CricLab corrects for this as far as the footage
allows, but it cannot recover what was never visible.

Slow motion is not the cause — CricLab works out the real capture rate itself.

## "Release looks like it happened at the wrong moment"

Send the clip through support. Include the delivery reference and say which
frame you think it should be. This is exactly the kind of report that improves
things.

## "My clip will not upload"

- Keep it under about thirty seconds.
- MP4 and MOV are the safest formats.
- On a weak connection, wait for wifi — a large 4K file is a lot to push over
  mobile data.

## "Processing is taking a long time"

Longer, larger and higher frame rate clips take longer. If a job has been going
for much longer than others of the same size, open a support ticket with the
reference.

## "I did not get the confirmation email"

Check spam. Codes last ten minutes, so ask for a fresh one if it has been
longer. If nothing arrives at all, the address may have a typo — sign up again
with the correct one, or open a ticket.
